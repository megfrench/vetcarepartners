FAR of the Month

Volume IV
FAR Part 16: Types of Contracts

Specifically: Indefinite-Delivery Contracts (IDIQ)

The Jargon: "Indefinite-delivery contracts are used when the exact times and/or exact quantities of future deliveries are not known at the time of contract award..."

The Legion Translation: The government knows they need something, but they don't know exactly how much or when. An IDIQ (Indefinite Delivery, Indefinite Quantity) establishes the pricing and terms up front. Then, the government issues individual "Task Orders" (for services) or "Delivery Orders" (for products) against that master contract as needs arise.

Why you care: Winning a spot on an IDIQ doesn't guarantee you money (except a tiny minimum). It gives you a hunting license. You still have to compete against the other winners for the actual task orders. Don't pop the champagne just for getting on the vehicle; prepare to hustle for the orders.
