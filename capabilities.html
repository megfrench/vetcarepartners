Specialized Education & Training

Equipping Care Teams.
Elevating Outcomes.

Veteran Care Partners provides comprehensive, turnkey educational modules designed to empower healthcare agencies and their personnel with the specialized knowledge needed to excel.

Our Educational Modules

We don't compete with your care network; we enhance it. VCP provides turnkey, comprehensive training materials designed to close the knowledge gap for your caregivers, ensuring compliant, empathetic, and effective care delivery.

Military Culture Modules

Bridge the gap between civilian caregivers and veteran patients. Our comprehensive training covers military terminology, cultural norms, common physical/invisible wounds, and how to build trust with those who have served.

Working in Home Healthcare as an Immigrant

Empower your immigrant workforce with the knowledge they need to thrive. This module addresses workplace cultural nuances, communication strategies, and navigating the US home healthcare system as a professional caregiver.

Navigating Medicare & Medicaid

Demystify complex government healthcare systems for your staff and the families they serve. We provide clear, actionable materials on eligibility, coverage limitations, billing basics, and patient advocacy.

VA Pension Aid & Attendance

A critical resource for agencies serving senior veterans. This training details the eligibility requirements and application process for VA Aid & Attendance, helping families unlock funds to pay for essential home care.

Engaging with VSOs

Learn how to effectively partner with Veteran Service Officers (VSOs). We teach your staff how to locate local advocates, understand their roles, and facilitate warm handoffs for patients needing claims assistance.

Custom Module Development

Need specific training for a unique contract or patient demographic? Our Subject Matter Experts (SMEs) can develop bespoke educational materials and compliance training tailored exactly to your agency's operational needs.

Everything Optum-ized: Securing an Optum CCN Deal

Unlock access to the VA Community Care Network. We break down the exact steps, requirements, and compliance metrics needed to secure and maintain a lucrative contract with Optum.

How to Source High Quality Nursing and Care Teams

Discover proven strategies for recruiting, vetting, and retaining elite caregivers. This module provides actionable insights into building a reliable, highly skilled home health workforce that stands out.

Better Understanding B2G Partnerships in Healthcare

Demystify the Business-to-Government space. Learn how to navigate federal frameworks, identify strategic teaming opportunities, and position your agency for long-term success in government contracting.
