Generations of Service

Born Into Duty.
Raised By Honor.

At Veteran Care Partners, our commitment to serving those who served isn't just a corporate mission statement—it is written in the DNA of our founder, Megan French, the "Meg" of Meg and Greg Group LLC. Discover the generational legacy that built our foundation.

Our Foundation

A Legacy Woven in Uniform

To understand why Veteran Care Partners demands the highest standard of honor and candor in our contracting, you have to understand where our founder, Megan French, comes from. This isn't just a business; it's a profound continuation of her family's service.

The Call of the Navy

Megan's story begins before she was even born. While her mother was pregnant, her biological father answered the call to serve, departing for duty in the United States Navy. His own father had paved the way before him as an Army veteran, setting an early standard of sacrifice.

It was in this environment of duty that the foundation for VCP was laid. Megan was raised surrounded by the profound understanding of what it means when a family member leaves to serve their country.

A Lineage of Army and Air Force Vets

This dedication to service was not isolated to one side of the family. Megan's mother’s side was equally steeped in military tradition. Her grandfather was an Army veteran, and his father before him also served in the Army.

The legacy expanded through the branches. Megan's great uncle served in the Air Force, and her maternal grandmother’s husband was also a proud veteran of the USAF.

Surrounded by Strength

Needless to say, Megan's entire life has been shaped by the presence of strong, well-respected men who put their country first. But equally important were the women who raised her—women of immense resilience and fortitude who managed the home front, supported their loved ones, and instilled in her an unbreakable moral compass.

"I was raised by honor, surrounded by duty. I understand the veteran experience not through a textbook, but through my lineage."

— Megan French, Founder

Translating Respect into Action

Veteran Care Partners is the manifestation of profound respect for Megan's family history. When we bridge the gap between the VA and top-tier home health agencies, we do so with the strict requirement that every veteran is treated with the ultimate dignity, candor, and honor they have earned.
