About VCP

Elevating the Standard of Veteran Home Healthcare Through Education.

Veteran Care Partners (VCP) recognizes a critical gap in the private home health sector: a lack of specialized, culturally competent training for caregivers treating the veteran population. We are dedicated to filling that void.

We develop robust, evidence-based educational modules designed to be distributed through major health networks and directly to home health agencies. Our goal is to ensure that caregivers are not just medically proficient, but are equipped to handle the unique physical, emotional, and psychological nuances of military veterans.

"We empower agencies to deliver exceptional, dignified care by providing the specialized knowledge required to truly understand the veteran experience."

Core Capabilities

Strategic Educational Solutions

We leverage our deep understanding of military culture and healthcare requirements to build robust, scalable training programs.

Curriculum Development

Creating evidence-based, peer-reviewed training modules focused on the specific needs of the veteran population.

B2B Distribution Networks

Partnering with major organizations like USAging and ASAging, as well as prime contractors like Optum, to disseminate training to downstream agencies.

Automated Certification

Providing seamless, automated credentialing for agency staff upon completion of VCP training modules, ensuring compliance records are easily maintained.

Cultural Competence Focus

Equipping care teams with specialized knowledge regarding military culture, rank structures, and service-related trauma triggers.

Candor & Honor Training

Instilling a deep respect for the veteran experience within caregivers, ensuring care is delivered with the dignity our heroes deserve.
