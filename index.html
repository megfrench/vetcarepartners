About VCP

The Bridge Between Government Standards and Elite Home Care.

Veteran Care Partners (VCP) operates as the vital connective tissue between the Department of Veterans Affairs and America's premier home health agencies. We understand the complex landscape of federal contracting and the profound responsibility of caring for our nation's heroes.

Our care teams are not just medically proficient; they are specially chosen for their candor, honor, and deep respect for those who have served. By providing specialized training in interacting with veterans, we ensure that every interaction honors their sacrifice.

"We eliminate friction in the contracting process so that agencies can focus on what they do best: delivering exceptional, dignified care to veterans."

A Symbol of Trust

Backed by the robust operational excellence and corporate infrastructure of Meg and Greg Group LLC.
