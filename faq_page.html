Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about partnering with Veteran Care Partners, our SME deployment processes, and our federal compliance standards.

Veteran Care Partners (VCP) acts as the critical bridge between the Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) and premier private home health agencies. We are a B2G (Business-to-Government) contracting entity.

We win and manage complex federal healthcare contracts, and then deploy highly vetted, culturally competent private healthcare teams to deliver the actual in-home care to veterans. This allows the care agencies to focus entirely on patients without worrying about federal red tape, SAM.gov compliance, or DCAA accounting standards.

No, we are not a government agency. Veteran Care Partners is a private, commercial entity and a flagship division of the Meg and Greg Group LLC. We operate as an authorized federal contractor that provides services to the government.

We are headquartered in Columbus, Ohio alongside our parent company, Meg and Greg Group LLC. However, our capabilities and contract deployments are nationwide. We partner with agencies and hire SMEs across the entire United States.

In accordance with FAR Subpart 9.6, we establish formal teaming agreements with private home health or logistics agencies. Typically, VCP acts as the Prime Contractor—absorbing the administrative burden, proposal writing, and compliance liability.

Our partners act as Subcontractors, deploying their staff to fulfill the specialized requirements of the contract. This creates a zero-friction environment for our partners, allowing them to access federal revenue streams without building an internal GovCon department.

If you are serving as a formal subcontractor on a federal award, yes, you will eventually need a Unique Entity Identifier (UEI) and an active SAM.gov registration. However, if your agency is new to the federal space, our administrative team at Meg and Greg Group LLC can assist you in navigating the registration process as part of our teaming agreement.

Once your application is submitted via our Teaming Arrangements page, our Acquisitions team will review your agency's profile. If your capabilities align with our upcoming federal pipeline, we will reach out to schedule an initial capability briefing and send you a mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) before discussing specific solicitations.

The Subject Matter Expert (SME) Pool is our proprietary network of highly qualified military veterans. When VCP or our sister brands (like Lodging Sources) bid on complex federal or commercial contracts, we often require specialized oversight, project management, or clinical expertise.

Instead of posting these roles publicly right away, we recruit directly from our SME pool on a contingency or project basis, ensuring that veterans get first priority on leadership roles in the private sector.

No. While having an active or recent clearance (Secret, TS/SCI) makes you highly competitive for certain Department of Defense or Intelligence Community contracts, many of our VA healthcare logistics and event operations roles simply require a standard background check and drug screening.

The Digital Legion is our free monthly email dispatch. It provides actionable GovCon (Government Contracting) intelligence, exclusive job postings from the Hiring Board, and breakdowns of complex FAR regulations. It is designed specifically for veterans and contractors navigating the B2G space. You can subscribe via the Newsletter page.

No, the Intel Forum is not a real government website. It is a private, anonymous community board hosted by the Meg and Greg Group ecosystem.

It was created as a space for contractors, healthcare professionals, and SMEs to share unvarnished insights regarding federal solicitations and industry operations. Due to the sensitive nature of GovCon discussions, the forum employs an Ephemeral Data Protocol—all posts self-destruct from public view after 30 days. Please note that posting classified material (SECRET, TS/SCI) on the forum is strictly prohibited and is an immediate OPSEC violation.
