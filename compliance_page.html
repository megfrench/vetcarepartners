Code of Business Ethics and Conduct

Veteran Care Partners (VCP), a subsidiary brand of Meg and Greg Group LLC, is committed to conducting business with the highest level of integrity, honor, and compliance with all applicable laws and regulations. Our core mission—serving those who have served—demands that we operate transparently and ethically in every facet of our organization.

This Code applies to all employees, independent contractors, teaming partners, Subject Matter Experts (SMEs), and suppliers operating under the VCP umbrella. We mandate zero tolerance for bribery, corruption, or the exploitation of federal funds.

Federal Contracting & FAR Compliance

As an active participant in Business-to-Government (B2G) contracting, VCP adheres strictly to the Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) and the Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement (DFARS) where applicable.

DCAA Accounting Standards

Financial integrity is paramount. VCP utilizes accounting systems designed to be fully compliant with Defense Contract Audit Agency (DCAA) guidelines. This ensures proper segregation of direct and indirect costs, accurate timekeeping protocols, and unallowable cost exclusion as defined in FAR Part 31.

Improper Business Practices (FAR Part 3)

VCP explicitly prohibits any practices that violate FAR Part 3, including but not limited to:

  • Offering or accepting kickbacks (Anti-Kickback Act of 1986).
  • Conflicts of interest regarding federal employees or former federal employees.
  • Collusive bidding or anti-competitive practices.

Healthcare Compliance & HIPAA

In our capacity coordinating care between the Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) and private home health agencies, VCP handles sensitive health data. We are fully committed to the requirements of the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) and the Health Information Technology for Economic and Clinical Health (HITECH) Act.

Protection of PHI

All Protected Health Information (PHI) is encrypted at rest and in transit. Access to PHI is strictly limited based on the Principle of Least Privilege. Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) are executed and enforced with all downstream home health agencies, sub-contractors, and software vendors before any data exchange occurs.

OPSEC & Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI)

Operations Security (OPSEC) is critical to our operations. Information generated by or provided to VCP by the federal government may be designated as Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI).

NIST SP 800-171 & DoD Instruction 5200.48

We mandate the safeguarding of all CUI in accordance with DoD Instruction 5200.48 and the security requirements outlined in NIST SP 800-171. Our internal systems, communication channels, and teaming portals utilize multi-factor authentication (MFA) and data loss prevention (DLP) protocols.

Notice regarding VCP Portals: Unauthorized uploading, sharing, or transmission of Classified Data (SECRET, TS/SCI, NOFORN) or improperly redacted CUI to public-facing VCP platforms (including the Intel Forum) is a severe violation of federal law and will result in immediate reporting to the appropriate federal authorities.

Employment Policies & EEO

Equal Employment Opportunity

Meg and Greg Group LLC and Veteran Care Partners are Equal Opportunity Employers. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, disability, or veteran status. While we actively prioritize the hiring of veterans through our SME Preferential Pool, all employment decisions are based on merit, qualifications, and business needs.

Fair Chance Act

In alignment with federal directives, VCP supports fair chance hiring practices. Criminal history inquiries are delayed until later stages of the recruitment process, except where explicitly required by the nature of the federal contract or specific security clearance requirements.

Reporting & Whistleblower Protection

VCP relies on its employees, partners, and contractors to uphold our commitment to integrity. We maintain a strict, zero-tolerance policy against retaliation of any kind directed at individuals who, in good faith, report suspected violations of the law, the FAR, or our internal Code of Conduct.

How to Report an Issue:

If you suspect a compliance violation, ethical breach, or mishandling of PHI/CUI, you are encouraged to report it immediately to our Corporate Compliance Officer.
