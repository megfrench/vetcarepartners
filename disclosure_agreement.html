Disclosure Agreement

Corporate affiliations, data sharing policies, organizational conflicts of interest (OCI), and confidentiality guidelines for the VCP ecosystem.

Corporate Affiliations Data & Information Sharing Conflict of Interest (OCI) Confidentiality & NDA Financial Disclosures

1. Corporate Affiliations & Structure

Veteran Care Partners (VCP) operates as a dedicated division and flagship brand under the corporate umbrella of Meg and Greg Group LLC, an Ohio-based limited liability company. Meg and Greg Group LLC serves as the central administrative, compliance, and legal hub for all VCP operations.

Sister Brands & Ecosystem

Users of this website should be aware that VCP shares corporate infrastructure, administrative resources, and recruiting pipelines with its sister brand, Lodging Sources. When you apply for positions via our Hiring Board or submit your credentials to the Veteran SME Preferential Pool, your information is processed centrally by Meg and Greg Group LLC.

This shared infrastructure allows us to efficiently deploy personnel across both home healthcare logistics (via VCP) and broader event/accommodation logistics (via Lodging Sources) for federal and commercial clients.

2. Data & Information Sharing

By utilizing the interactive features of the VCP website—specifically the Hiring Board, the SME Network Application, and the Digital Legion Newsletter—you consent to the collection and internal sharing of your professional data within the Meg and Greg Group ecosystem.

SME Preferential Pool

Resumes, military histories, and areas of expertise submitted to the SME Pool are stored securely and evaluated continuously against upcoming federal solicitations and private sector needs. We do not sell your personal data to third-party data brokers. However, your profile may be shared under strict confidentiality with:

  • Federal Contracting Officers (COs) during the proposal evaluation phase (as Key Personnel).
  • Prime Contractors with whom Meg and Greg Group LLC has executed formal Teaming Agreements or Subcontracting Plans.
  • Internal recruiters filling positions for Lodging Sources or other affiliated MGG entities.

3. Organizational Conflict of Interest (OCI)

In strict accordance with FAR Subpart 9.5, Meg and Greg Group LLC and Veteran Care Partners proactively identify, evaluate, and mitigate any potential Organizational Conflicts of Interest (OCI).

Contractor & SME Obligations

All independent contractors, SME network members, and teaming partners engaging with VCP must immediately disclose any personal or organizational relationships that could constitute a conflict of interest. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Current or recent employment with the Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) or other relevant federal agencies.
  • Financial interests in competing healthcare or logistics firms bidding on the same solicitations.
  • Access to non-public source selection information related to federal procurements VCP is pursuing.

Failure to disclose a known conflict of interest is grounds for immediate termination of teaming agreements and removal from the SME Preferential Pool.

4. Confidentiality & NDA Requirements

Participation in specific VCP opportunities—including certain contingency-based federal roles and proprietary teaming agreements—requires the execution of a formal Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) prior to the exchange of detailed solicitation requirements or pricing strategies.

Intel Forum & Digital Legion

The information provided in the "Intel Forum" and the "Digital Legion Newsletter" is provided for general strategic and educational purposes. While we strive for accuracy, users must not upload, share, or disseminate Classified Data or improperly redacted Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) on any VCP platform. Users are solely responsible for their own OPSEC (Operations Security) and adherence to their respective agency's data handling policies.

5. Financial & Contracting Disclosures

VCP operates with full financial transparency as required by federal contracting laws. We explicitly disclose that our revenue models involve acting as a Prime Contractor, Subcontractor, or Joint Venture partner on federal and commercial awards.

Anti-Kickback Compliance

In accordance with the Anti-Kickback Act of 1986, Meg and Greg Group LLC does not offer, provide, accept, or solicit any compensation, fee, or gratuity for the purpose of improperly obtaining or rewarding favorable treatment in connection with a prime contract or a subcontract relating to a prime contract.
