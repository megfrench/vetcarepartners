Data Protection

Privacy Policy

How Veteran Care Partners collects, uses, secures, and protects your personal and professional information across our digital ecosystem.

1. Information We Collect 2. How We Use Your Data 3. Information Sharing 4. Security & Retention 5. Cookies & Tracking 6. Your Privacy Rights

1. Information We Collect

Veteran Care Partners ("VCP," "we," "us," or "our"), a division of Meg and Greg Group LLC, collects information to provide better services to our teaming partners, independent contractors, and the veteran community. The types of information we collect include:

Information You Provide to Us

  • SME Pool Applications: Name, contact details, military service history, branch, clearance level, professional expertise, resumes/CVs, and bios submitted via our SME application form.
  • Teaming Arrangements: Corporate entity names, DBAs, UEI numbers, headquarters locations, primary POC details, and capability statements submitted via our partnership forms.
  • Communications: Email addresses provided for the Digital Legion Newsletter or any correspondence directed to our contact or legal email addresses.

Information We Collect Automatically

When you visit our Site, we automatically collect certain technical data, including your IP address, browser type, operating system, pages visited, referring URLs, and timestamps. Note: IP addresses utilized in the Intel Forum are hashed for tripcode generation and are not stored in raw format, nor are they linked to your personally identifiable information (PII).

2. How We Use Your Data

The information we collect is used to operate our business, evaluate partnerships, and fulfill federal contracting obligations. Specifically, we use your data to:

  • Evaluate your agency's capabilities for potential Prime/Sub Teaming Agreements on federal solicitations.
  • Match your profile in the Veteran SME Preferential Pool with upcoming contingency, project-based, or full-time roles across the Meg and Greg Group ecosystem.
  • Send you the Digital Legion Newsletter, industry updates, and direct notifications regarding relevant job postings (if you have opted in).
  • Analyze website usage to improve the functionality, security, and user experience of our digital portals.
  • Comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our Terms of Service and Teaming Agreements.

3. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party data brokers. However, to execute our B2G operations, your data may be shared under strict confidentiality in the following circumstances:

Internal MGG Ecosystem

Your information is stored centrally and shared securely with Meg and Greg Group LLC and its subsidiaries (including Lodging Sources) for the purpose of cross-functional recruiting and logistics coordination.

Federal Agencies & Contracting Officers

If you are selected from the SME Pool for a specific federal bid, your professional qualifications, resume, and clearance status may be submitted to federal Contracting Officers (COs) or Prime Contractors as "Key Personnel" documentation, as required by the FAR.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, in response to a subpoena, court order, or federal audit (such as DCAA), or when necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of VCP, our users, or the public.

4. Data Security & Retention

We implement robust, industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal information. As a federal contractor, we align our data protection practices with NIST SP 800-171 guidelines for safeguarding Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI), though general website data is not classified as CUI.

Retention of Corporate & SME Data

Teaming applications, corporate documents, and SME Pool profiles are retained securely in our active database to continuously match partners with pipeline opportunities. You may request the deletion of your SME profile at any time by contacting our legal department.

The Intel Forum: Ephemeral Data Protocol

To protect operational security (OPSEC) and encourage the free exchange of unclassified industry intelligence, the "Intel Forum" operates on a strict ephemeral protocol. All dispatches, metadata, and uploaded attachments on the Intel Forum automatically self-destruct from public view and are purged thirty (30) days after posting. We do not maintain historical archives of forum activity.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies to track activity on our Site and hold certain information. Cookies are files with a small amount of data which may include an anonymous unique identifier.

You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. However, if you do not accept cookies, you may not be able to use some portions of our Site, such as maintaining persistent session settings across our job boards.

6. Your Privacy Rights & Choices

Depending on your location (such as under the CCPA or GDPR, if applicable), you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, including the right to access, correct, update, or request deletion of your personal data.

Opting Out of Communications

You may opt out of receiving the Digital Legion Newsletter or other promotional emails from us at any time by following the unsubscribe link provided in those emails, or by contacting us directly.

Contacting the Privacy Officer

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, wish to request a copy of the data we hold on you, or wish to be removed from the SME Preferential Pool, please contact the Meg and Greg Group LLC Legal Department.
