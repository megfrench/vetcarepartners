Our Philosophy

Vetting for Character, Not Just Capability.

In the realm of home healthcare, medical proficiency is the baseline expectation. But when treating America's veterans, the baseline is simply not enough. The VCP Difference lies in our unyielding insistence on candor, honor, and character.

When we forge teaming agreements with established home health agencies, we look beyond their clinical certifications. We evaluate their culture. We demand that the caregivers deployed on our federal contracts possess a genuine reverence for the sacrifices made by their patients.

A veteran’s home is their sanctuary. The care they receive within it must be delivered with the utmost dignity, respecting the rank they held, the burdens they carried, and the service they gave.

The Standard of Honor

  • Treating every veteran with profound respect, regardless of age or condition.

  • Honesty and transparency in all caregiver-patient interactions (Candor).

  • Upholding the dignity of the uniform, even long after it has been taken off.

Building Ecosystems of Care

We don't just fill contracts. We engineer dedicated support networks tailored specifically to the unique physical, emotional, and psychological needs of the veteran community.

Cultural Competence

Our partnered care teams are trained to understand the nuances of military culture. This includes awareness of service-related traumas, PTSD triggers, and the stoic nature often inherent in those who have served, ensuring care is never invasive or misunderstood.

Zero-Friction Operations

By leveraging Meg and Greg Group LLC to handle the heavy burden of federal compliance, SAM.gov registration, and B2G administration, we allow the home health agencies to focus 100% of their energy on what matters: the patient.

Veteran SME Integration

Our Veteran Subject Matter Experts (SMEs) are critical to our mission. They help us to develop and provide free educational training modules to our partnered home health agencies, guaranteeing culturally competent care. Furthermore, we proudly prioritize our SME network for preferential contingency hiring across all federal procurement operations.
