Our Core Competencies

Backed by the robust corporate infrastructure of Meg and Greg Group LLC, we bring comprehensive, turnkey solutions to the Department of Veterans Affairs and private healthcare agencies alike.

Federal Contracting (B2G)

We seamlessly navigate complex government Requests for Proposals (RFPs), managing the logistical and administrative bridges between federal agencies like the VA and the private health sector. We serve as a highly capable Prime Contractor.

Talent Sourcing & Vetting

Not all care is created equal. We identify and partner with established, trusted home health agencies. We vet care providers specifically for their candor, honor, and capability to serve those who have served.

Strategic Teaming Agreements

We structure equitable, compliant teaming arrangements with top-tier healthcare agencies. This allows agencies to focus purely on patient care while we handle the federal compliance and contract management overhead.

Compliance Frameworks

Leveraging MGG's infrastructure ensures strict adherence to federal standards. We handle SAM.gov registrations, FAR clause management, DCAA compliance, and rigorous legal structuring.

Veteran SME Deployment

Through our specialized network, we source and deploy Veteran Subject Matter Experts (SMEs) on a contingency basis for specific logistical, administrative, and clinical oversight roles on federal contracts.

Care Logistics Brokerage

Beyond personnel, we provide turnkey logistical coordination. This includes the supply chain management of specialized medical equipment and operational readiness for complex, multi-state contracts.

The MGG Advantage

Shared Infrastructure, Specialized Execution.

Veteran Care Partners does not operate in a vacuum. As a flagship brand of Meg and Greg Group LLC, we utilize a centralized administrative hub that handles HR, payroll, complex accounting, and IT.

This unique corporate structure allows our subsidiary operations and teaming partners to stay incredibly lean, agile, and 100% focused on their primary mission: delivering unparalleled care to the veteran community.
